# Reference site audit: ejcars.co.uk

Checked on 15 Nov 2026. The site was built by **Dragon2000** (a UK dealer-website platform, WordPress based). It sells cars only, with no rentals. It specialises in used hybrid and EV cars in Stoke-on-Trent and had about 116 vehicles in stock.

## Pages / navigation
Home · Showroom (`/used-cars-for-sale/`) · Warranty · Finance · About Us · Careers · Contact Us.
Other pages: Vehicle detail (`/vehicle-details/<slug>/`), Valuation (`/valuation/`), Shortlist/Favourites (`/shortlist/`), Privacy, Cookies, Complaints Policy, Initial Disclosure Document, Sitemap.

## Header
- Two phone numbers (mobile and landline), "Call Us" button, favourites counter (heart icon with count).
- Sticky menu, with a hamburger menu on mobile.

## Homepage (in order)
1. Hero with a welcome tagline ("Hybrid & Electric Vehicle Specialists") and a stock count ("X cars in stock").
2. Quick search: Make, Model, a toggle between Cash Price and Monthly Price, Max price, and a Search button.
3. "Latest Vehicles" carousel (Swiper). Each card shows a Featured badge, sales status, make/model/variant, price, "or from £X p/m", and a More details button.
4. SEO intro text.
5. Four link tiles: About Us, Our Stock, Warranty, Contact Us.
6. "Explore our range" text block.
7. "Drive With Confidence" warranty promotion (Evolution Warranties, Advance Package).
8. "Why choose us" section.
9. Footer.

## Showroom / stock listing
- **Filters:** Manufacturer, Model, Trim, Price (cash or monthly), Year from/to, Mileage, Transmission, Fuel type, Body type, Doors, Power from/to, Colour, ULEZ compliance. There are also "Clear all" and a result count.
- **Sort:** Latest arrivals, Price low/high, Monthly price low/high, Age, Mileage.
- **Card:** photos, price, discount ("OFF") badge, "or from £X p/m" (or "call us for finance"), and a Representative Example popup (deposit, term, APR, total payable).
- A "View more" button loads further results (no numbered pages).

## Vehicle detail page
- Continue search, Print PDF, Share (WhatsApp, Facebook, X, Email, copy link).
- Urgency badge: "Popular / Hurry, X people have viewed this car today".
- Gallery (69 photos) and a **Watch Video** button.
- Key specs: year, transmission, fuel, mileage, colour.
- Price box with two tabs: **Pay in full** and **Pay monthly** (finance quote, or a "call showroom" fallback).
- Calls to action: **Reserve now with a £99 refundable deposit, held for 24 hours** (online payment), Make enquiry, Book a test drive.
- Part-exchange quote: enter a registration and click Get a quote.
- Spec grid: 0–62 time, doors, engine size, BHP, CO2.
- Long description and feature lists.
- Tabs: Fitted Extras, Technical Specs (performance, battery range and capacity, emissions/ULEZ, weight, dimensions), Vehicle Equipment.
- "You may also like" (similar cars).
- Enquiry form: name, email, phone, part exchange yes/no (registration and mileage if yes), message, and reCAPTCHA.
- Sticky bottom bar on mobile: car name, price, Enquire and Reserve Now buttons.

## Forms / lead capture
- **Valuation (Value my car):** two steps. Step 1 is the registration lookup, which auto-fills make, model, year, fuel and engine, then mileage, service history and owners. Step 2 is name, email, phone and comments.
- **Contact:** name, email, phone, message. The page also shows the address, phones, email and opening hours.
- Vehicle enquiry, test drive and reserve forms. All forms use reCAPTCHA.
- No live chat, WhatsApp chat widget or newsletter was found.

## Finance
- Credit broker introducer (Admiral is mentioned), 12.9% representative APR.
- The page explains what car finance is and how it works.
- Full FCA disclosure is in the footer.

## Footer / legal
- Phones, address, opening hours (Mon–Sat 9–5, Sunday by appointment), and a vehicle quick search.
- Company number, VAT number, registered office, FCA appointed representative number (FRN) with the principal firm, ICO registration number.
- Cookie consent banner with Accept, Settings and Deny options.

## Design
- **Colours:** navy `#233F52` and steel blue `#345F7D` (primary), light blue accent `#6ABFFC`, light grey background `#F4F5F6`, dark `#0A0A0A` / `#1C2527`, white.
- **Fonts:** Poppins (300/400/600/700), with Montserrat in places. A UK number-plate font is used for registration inputs. Icons are Font Awesome 6.
- Clean, corporate, template look. Carousels use Swiper.

## Weak points we can do better
- The Warranty page shows finance text (a content bug).
- A placeholder bug on the homepage prints the business name as "6".
- There are no customer reviews or ratings (Google or AutoTrader) anywhere.
- The template-generic design has weak brand identity.
- There is no live chat or WhatsApp button.
- Cars are for sale only. **YM Cars needs a full Rentals section as well.**

## Features to carry over to YM Cars Ltd (Sales)
Quick search, stock filters and sorting, cash/monthly price toggle, vehicle cards with p/m pricing, a detail page with gallery, video, specs tabs, finance quote, reserve deposit, enquiry, test drive, part-exchange lookup and share, a two-step valuation form, favourites/shortlist, Print PDF, similar cars, sticky mobile CTA bar, FCA/ICO/company footer, cookie consent.
